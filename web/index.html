Choosing Number

Solitaire

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • One player. Pick your bet and press Play.
  • Move all 52 cards onto the 4 piles (A to K) to win.
  • A win pays 2x your bet.
  • You have 10 minutes. If time ends or you quit, the bet is lost.
  • The cards are dealt and checked by the server.

8 Ball Pool

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • FREE DEMO: no money, no deposits, no prizes.
  • 4 players on one table. Highest score wins.
  • Pocket a ball: +1 point (the 8-ball +2) and shoot again.
  • Foul: -1 point and the next player has the cue ball in hand.
  • 10 minutes per match, 20 seconds per shot.

RULES

HOW TO WIN

Move all 52 cards onto the 4 piles (by suit) from Ace to King.

GETTING STARTED

1. Choose your bet (minimum 30 ETB) and press ▶.

2. Cards are dealt face down in 7 columns (1 to 7 cards); the top card of each is face up. The rest is the deck (draw 3).

HOW TO PLAY

1. Build the 4 piles in ascending order, Ace to King, same suit.

2. In the columns build downward with alternating colors (a red 7 on a black 8).

3. Drag one card or a group to another column.

4. Only a King (or a group starting with a King) can go on an empty column.

5. If you cannot move any more, press "Give up".

6. Win = your bet x2.

7. Playing on two tables at once is not allowed.

A game must be finished within 10 minutes, otherwise the bet is lost. Problems? Contact support within 3 days.
