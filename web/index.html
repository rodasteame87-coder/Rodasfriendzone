Bingo

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • 75 numbers are called one by one.
  • Pick a cartela before the round starts.
  • Complete a pattern: line, diagonal or corners.
  • Press BINGO before the next call, or leave Auto ON.
  • The winner takes 80% of all bets.

Choosing Number

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • Choose a room: 50 or 100 birr per number.
  • You can own up to 5 numbers in a room.
  • The winners (gold and silver, and bronze in some rooms) are drawn and locked before the countdown.
  • Anyone can check the draw with the published hash.
  • Prizes go to your balance automatically.

Solitaire Multiplayer

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • 2 to 4 players get the same Solitaire deal. Each plays alone on his own board.
  • Pick a bet. The match starts at once with 4 players, or after the wait time with 2 or 3 players. The prize is a share of the bets of the players who play.
  • You can turn the deck over as many times as you want, but every board has a move limit.
  • Win: first to put all 52 cards on the piles, or the most cards on the piles when the time ends.
  • The match ends at once when a player puts all 52 cards on the piles, or when nobody can win any more.
  • Tie: the player who reached that number of cards first wins.
  • Fewer than 2 players in time, or nobody put a card on the piles: everybody gets the bet back.
  • The cards are dealt and checked by the server.

8 Ball Pool

Rules & Regulations ▾

  • FREE DEMO: no money, no deposits, no prizes.
  • 4 players on one table. Highest score wins.
  • Pocket a ball: +1 point (the 8-ball +2) and shoot again.
  • Foul: -1 point and the next player has the cue ball in hand.
  • 10 minutes per match, 20 seconds per shot.

MY GAMES

RULES

HOW TO WIN

Move all 52 cards onto the 4 piles (by suit) from Ace to King.

GETTING STARTED

1. Choose your bet (minimum 30 ETB) and press ▶.

2. Cards are dealt face down in 7 columns (1 to 7 cards); the top card of each is face up. The rest is the deck (draw 3).

HOW TO PLAY

1. Build the 4 piles in ascending order, Ace to King, same suit.

2. In the columns build downward with alternating colors (a red 7 on a black 8).

3. Drag one card or a group to another column.

4. Only a King (or a group starting with a King) can go on an empty column.

5. If you cannot move any more, press "Give up".

6. Win = your bet x2.

7. Playing on two tables at once is not allowed.

Problems? Contact support within 3 days.
